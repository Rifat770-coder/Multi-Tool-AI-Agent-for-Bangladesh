# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Critique ideas rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention and publishing private information are unacceptable in issues, pull requests and project spaces.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated or severe misconduct. They should consider context, explain decisions privately when possible, and handle reports confidentially within practical limits. Appeals should be reviewed by an uninvolved maintainer when one is available.

Unresolved maintainer action: publish a confirmed private reporting contact. No private reporting channel has been verified. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. This is a project-specific policy, not a claimed adoption of an external template.
